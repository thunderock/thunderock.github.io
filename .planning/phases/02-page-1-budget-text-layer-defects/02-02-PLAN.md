## T-1 — `EDU_CITY` after the city cells are gone

**Measured today** (`pdftotext` default mode, gate lines 99-112): *both* Education rows
extract their **right cell first** — `Bloomington, IN` (100) before `Indiana University,
Bloomington` (102), and `Patna, India` (107) before `National Institute of Technology`
(109). Work Experience rows extract left-cell-first. Extraction order of a two-cell row is
therefore measured, never predicted.

**Consequence D-09 must be planned around:** D-09 moves the date to the header row's right
cell. On today's measured behaviour that is the cell that extracts first, so the date may
simply replace the city as the string a parser reads before the school.

**Rejected: repoint `EDU_CITY` at the degree line.** It needs no harness edit, and it is a
false PASS generator — with the date extracting first, "institution precedes degree" is
still true, so `G6.12` would go green on a document that still leads with a non-institution
line. That silently un-asserts the defect the region scoping was built to catch.

**Rejected: disable the ordering clause when the anchor is absent.** That is weakening.

**Adopted: make the clause anchor-free and stronger — the institution must be the FIRST
non-empty extracted line inside the EDUCATION region.** This encodes G6.12's purpose
("institution binds before location noise") directly, fires on city-first, date-first and
degree-first alike, needs no second anchor string, and removes the empty-needle trap by
construction. `EDU_CITY` is then deleted from the manifest **in the same commit** that
deletes its only reader. The window clause on `EDU_DATE`/`EDU_BIND_WINDOW` is retained
unchanged. A negative control against the pre-change artifact proves the new clause is not
a tautology.
